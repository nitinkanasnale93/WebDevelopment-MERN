Nani

Google me Nani is an Indian actor and film producer who primarily works in Telugu cinema. He is known for his natural acting style and has appeared in films across different genres. Nani has established himself as one of the leading actors in Telugu cinema.

Career

Early career

Before becoming an actor, Nani worked as a radio jockey and later worked as an assistant director. He made his acting debut with the Telugu film Ashta Chamma in 2008.

Rise to popularity

Nani gained recognition through films such as Ala Modalaindi, Eega, Pilla Zamindar and Bhale Bhale Magadivoy. His natural performances helped him develop a strong connection with audiences.

Notable films

Some of Nani's well-known films include Eega, Bhale Bhale Magadivoy, Ninnu Kori, Jersey, Shyam Singha Roy, Dasara and Hi Nanna.

Personal life

Nani was born on 24 February 1984 in Hyderabad, Telangana. He is married to Anjana Yelavarthy and they have a son.
