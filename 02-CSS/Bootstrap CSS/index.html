Chapter 01 • The Beginning

BEFORE
THE CARS.

BMW's story begins with aviation-related engine production. Motorcycles followed in the 1920s, and automobile production became a major part of the company's identity.

This site follows that transformation instead of presenting BMW as if it had always been a car company.
